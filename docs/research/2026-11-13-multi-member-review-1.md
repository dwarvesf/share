Target: `DECISION-BRIEF-multi-member.md` (DRAFT) against `bin/share` at 4564c5c and Cloudflare docs. Cites: `L123` = bin/share line; `brief:N` = brief line.

| 1 | blocker | security, reconcile | Tombstones turn a bucket write into deletion of files on another machine: any publisher token can write `t/<id>` and DELETE `m/<id>`, and the owner's reconcile then trashes its own bytes and row. Today a forged DELETE only makes the origin rewrite the pointer; the brief adds a destructive remote trigger and applies it to "every install", the origin included. | A leaked invite (90-day publisher token) or a careless member script writes `t/` for every origin v:2 id and deletes the pointers; the admin's next `share prune` trashes every origin link. | On a tombstone, stop rewriting the pointer and mark the row "removed remotely" in `ls`; bytes go only on a local `rm` or local expiry. | brief:55, brief:152, brief:167; L2978 (today's reconcile only touches the pointer); ADR-0007 L38 |
| 2 | blocker | Cloudflare objects | The design omits the VPC Service object. A tunnel ingress rule is the public-hostname mechanism and plays no part in VPC routing. | `member add` step 3/4 cannot be built as written; `member rm` deletes binding and tunnel but leaves the VPC Service (and a tunnel delete may fail while a service references it); the exit criterion "no tunnel, token, binding, or pointer" passes with an orphan service. | Add the VPC Service as its own artifact in member add (create after tunnel, before deploy), rm (deploy without binding, then delete service, then connections, then tunnel), rotate, and the exit criteria; drop the ingress step. | brief:37, brief:101, brief:109, brief:222; https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-vpc/configuration/vpc-services/ ("register each target as a VPC Service"; binding keys `binding`, `service_id`) |
| 3 | blocker | die points, member add | `member add` does not converge on rerun. | 4 | major | concurrency | Every Worker deploy replaces the whole binding list (`keep_bindings` keeps only `secret_text`), so two admin commands from two shells lose an update.
