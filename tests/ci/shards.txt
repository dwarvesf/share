# CI shards for the bash suite. One line per shard: <name><TAB><extended regex for SHARE_TEST_ONLY>.
# tests/ci/shard-check.sh fails when a section runs in no shard or in two. It resolves the `# group` and `# pull` lines of
# tests/share.sh the way the suite does, so a shard that names one member of a group runs the whole group.
#
# chain: the core, r2, groundwork and late groups. `late` pulls the other three, and r2 and groundwork read core's
# variables (a run of either without core aborts or hangs), so they cannot be split. Its wall time is the floor of a PR run.
# The plain section `worker` rides along.
# members-a, members-b: the members sections that run alone, balanced by the timing table (about 235 s and 215 s on a Mac mini).
chain	^(members: phase 0|worker \()
members-a	^members: (member rm and member purge|revoke and reconcile|leave and the member|tenant T1 to T2|dry seams|pause, resume, invite)
members-b	^members: (member add|member machine|member ls and member sync|the registry|the admin lease|no token, key or code|Cloudflare object helpers|admin context|the invite seal)
